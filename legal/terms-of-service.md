## 1. What this is

BlueProof is a way for community monitors to record mangrove restoration work,
have it checked, and be paid for it. You photograph a plot, the system assesses
the photograph, and if it passes you are paid on M-Pesa.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be enrolled by us or by your Community Forest Association. If you are
under 18, a parent or guardian has to agree first and give us their name and
number.

## 3. What we ask of you

Photograph the plot you say you are photographing, on the day you say you are.
Report what you actually see. If you are not sure what a species is, say so:
the system is built to handle uncertainty and is not built to catch out someone
who guesses.

Do not submit a photograph of somebody else's plot, an old photograph, or an
image from anywhere but your own camera. Doing that is not a policy violation in
the abstract. It puts a false line into a record that other people rely on, and
it is the one thing that would make everything else here worthless.

## 4. What we owe you

**We pay KSh 150 for each verified submission.** If the amount ever changes, we
tell you before it does, not after.

**We pay within `[FILL: how long, realistically]` of verification.**

**We tell you why, every time.** If a submission does not pass, the app says
which of the three reasons applied: the model was not confident enough, the
condition could not be read, or the model disagreed with the species you
declared. You are never left guessing.

**We do not overrule you.** If the model reads a different species from the one
you declared, we do not change your answer. You stood at the plot and it did
not. The submission goes to a person to look at.

## 5. When a submission does not pass

It pays nothing, and we want to be straight about what that means: you did the
walk and took the photographs and got nothing for it. That is a real cost and we
know it falls on you.

We would rather that than the alternative. A system that pays out when it is not
sure produces a record nobody can trust, and the moment the record is not
trusted the money behind all of this goes away. So the system declines rather
than guesses, and it tells you plainly that declining is not an accusation.

If you think a decision was wrong, section 7 says what to do.

## 6. What happens to your work

A verified submission becomes a line in the stewardship record, which we show to
funders and may sell to carbon buyers. That is how the project is paid for.

That line does not have your name on it. It carries a reference. The privacy
notice explains why.

You keep whatever rights you have in your photographs. You give us permission to
use them to verify your submissions and to keep as evidence. We do not use your
photographs for advertising, we do not put them on social media, and we do not
sell them.

## 7. If you disagree with something

Tell your CFA lead, or contact us directly at `[FILL: contact]`.

**A verdict you think is wrong:** a person will look at the photograph and the
verdict, and you will get an answer within `[FILL: number] days`. If the review
finds in your favour you are paid, with no argument about it having been late.

**A payment that did not arrive:** tell us within 30 days. We will trace it with
Safaricom. If it failed on our side we send it again. If it failed because the
number we hold is wrong, we will fix the number and pay you.

**Anything else:** we will give you a written answer within 14 days. If you are
still not satisfied, nothing in these terms stops you going to the Office of the
Data Protection Commissioner, to a consumer body, or to court.

## 8. Refunds

There are none, because you never pay us anything. Money only moves from us to
you. If you were ever asked to pay for anything to use BlueProof, it did not
come from us.

## 9. Messages

We may send you an SMS or a WhatsApp message about a payment or a submission.

If you reply STOP we stop, and that applies from the first message. We do not
send marketing.

## 10. When this ends

You can leave whenever you want, and ask us to delete your details. We can end
it if you repeatedly submit photographs that are not of what you say they are,
and we will tell you why before doing so.

## 11. Honest limits

The verification system is new and has not been tested against real mangrove
photographs. It will get some things wrong. That is why a person reviews the
cases it is unsure about, why it declines rather than guesses, and why you can
challenge any decision. We are not going to pretend it is better than it is.

## 12. Law

Kenyan law. Kenyan courts.
