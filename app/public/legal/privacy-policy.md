## What we hold, and why

We hold four things about you, and nothing else.

**Your name.** So we can speak to you like a person, and so that if there is a
question about a submission we know whose it was.

**Your phone number.** It is your M-Pesa number. Without it we cannot pay you.

**Your Community Forest Association**, if you belong to one, so that the work
can be coordinated with the people you already work alongside.

**A record that you agreed to this**, and which version of this notice you
agreed to, and when. We keep that because if you ever ask us what you agreed to,
"we think you said yes" is not an answer.

If you are under 18, we also hold your parent or guardian's name and phone
number, because we will not hold a young person's information unless an adult
responsible for them has agreed to it.

## What we deliberately do not hold

**Your age or your date of birth.** We only ever need to know whether a guardian
has to agree. That is a yes or no, and it is all we store.

**Your national ID number.** We have no use for it. Do not send it to us.

**Where you are**, except the location of a plot at the moment you submit it.
The app does not track you and cannot see where you are at any other time.

**Anything from any other service.** We do not buy data about you, we are not
sent any, and the app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third party
code of any kind. It talks to our server and to nothing else.

## What happens to your work

When you submit a plot, the photograph and what the verifier concluded are
stored. If it passes, you are paid and a line is added to the stewardship
record, which is what funders and carbon buyers look at.

**Your name is not in that record.** It carries a reference like `BP-M-0007`
instead. We did that on purpose. The record already says which plot, which day,
and where to within a few metres. If your name were next to those three things,
anybody holding a copy of the record would know where you personally were on a
given morning, for every morning you worked. If you have reported cutting in
your own area, that is not a small thing.

Only we can connect `BP-M-0007` back to you, and we do it only to answer a
question about your own submissions or your own pay.

## Your photographs

Your photograph is the evidence, so we keep it while your submission is being
verified and while there is any chance of a dispute about it.

We have not yet decided how long that is, and rather than write a number here
that we made up, we are telling you that the decision is outstanding and will be
made before any real monitoring begins. When it is made, this notice will say
it.

Right now, photographs stay on our own server. If we later send them to an
outside company to be looked at by a model, we will tell you before we start,
and we will tell you which company and what they are allowed to do with them.
We will not send your photographs to a company that keeps them to train its own
systems.

## What you can ask us to do

**See everything we hold about you.** All of it, including the submissions that
did not pass and what the verifier said about them. Ask us and we will give you
the lot.

**Correct something wrong.** Tell us and we will fix it.

**Delete yourself.** We will erase your name, your number, your guardian's
details and your consent record. Not hidden, not flagged as deleted: removed.

Two things survive that, and we would rather tell you now than surprise you
later. Your stewardship records stay, under the reference, because they are the
record of work that was genuinely done and paid for and they no longer point at
anybody. And your payment records stay for seven years with the phone number
that was paid, because financial rules require it and we cannot lawfully delete
them.

**Withdraw your consent**, at any time, without explaining why. Nothing bad
happens to you for asking. It means we stop holding your details, which means we
can no longer pay you, so it ends the working relationship, but the choice is
entirely yours.

To do any of these, speak to your CFA lead or contact us at `[FILL: contact]`.
We will answer within seven days.

## What is kept on your phone

The app stores three things on the handset itself: the address of our server,
the plot you are currently working on, and any submission you made with no
network, held until it can be sent.

**The app sets no cookies at all.** Not "only the necessary ones": none. There
is nothing tracking you, nothing counting you, and nothing reporting what you do
to anybody.

You can clear all of it by clearing the site data in your browser. Anything
waiting to be sent will be lost if you do that before it syncs.

## If something goes wrong

If your information is exposed in a way that could harm you, we will tell you,
and we will tell the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours
of finding out. We will tell you what happened in plain words, not a notice that
takes a lawyer to read.

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects what we do with your information,
we will tell you and ask you to agree again. We will not change it quietly and
treat your silence as a yes.
